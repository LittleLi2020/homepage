论文简介 · Moral Hazard Spillovers Across LossesAbstract · Moral Hazard Spillovers Across Losses

Hong Li, Tao Li (Corresponding author) · Economics Letters (2026): 101–113

简要介绍Abstract

在一个竞争性保险市场中，考虑两种互斥的损失：其中一种存在道德风险，另一种不存在。我们证明：即使对不存在道德风险的那类损失，投保人也只会购买部分保险；且对后者的保障总是更为充足。数值分析显示，当预防努力更有效或投保人风险厌恶程度更低时，两类损失之间的保障差距会进一步扩大。

In a competitive insurance market with two mutually exclusive losses — one is subject to moral hazard while the other is not — we demonstrate that policyholders buy only partial insurance coverage for both losses, even if the second is free of moral hazard. Moreover, the coverage for the second loss is always more generous, and numerical analysis shows that the coverage gap widens with more efficient prevention efforts or lower risk aversion.
