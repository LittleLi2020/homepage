论文简介 · Falsification Fraud in Insurance Markets: Algorithms, Auditing, and NitpickingAbstract · Falsification Fraud in Insurance Markets: Algorithms, Auditing, and Nitpicking

Hong Li, Tao Li (Corresponding author) · The Journal of Risk and Insurance, accepted and forthcoming

简要介绍Abstract

本文研究保险市场中一种常见但研究不足的造假欺诈问题：投保人遭遇了合同免责范围内的真实损失，却将其谎报为承保损失。在一个审计策略有成本且无法事先承诺的完全竞争市场中，均衡状态下审计与造假行为并存；当审计成本较低或投保人风险厌恶程度较高时，均衡合同表现为保险不足，反之则表现为过度保险。文章进一步引入两种理赔处理工具：对报案理赔进行二元预审计评分，以及对被审计的真实理赔进行事后赔付削减（即"吹毛求疵式"理赔处理，nitpicking）。研究发现：算法驱动的定向审计对均衡的影响是非单调的——只有当算法灵敏度足够高时才能抑制欺诈并改善福利，否则反而会加剧造假、降低福利；赔付削减通过强化审计激励可以遏制造假，但同时削弱了对被审计真实理赔的保护；两种工具大体上互为替代，算法定向能力的提升会压缩赔付削减改善福利的空间。

This paper studies a prevailing yet underexplored problem of falsification fraud: a policyholder suffers a real loss in a contractually excluded state but reports it as covered. In a perfectly competitive market with a costly and uncommitted auditing strategy, equilibrium features positive auditing and falsification, and the equilibrium contract can exhibit underinsurance when auditing is inexpensive or the policyholder's risk aversion is high, and overinsurance otherwise. We then introduce two claims-handling tools: a binary pre-audit scoring algorithm on reported claims and post-audit payment reductions (nitpicking) on audited honest claims. We find that algorithm-induced targeted auditing has a non-monotonic equilibrium effect: it mitigates fraud and improves welfare only when sensitivity is sufficiently high; otherwise, it can raise falsification and reduce welfare. In addition, nitpicking can deter falsification by strengthening audit incentives, but it reduces protection for audited honest claims. Finally, the two tools operate largely as substitutes: improvements in algorithmic targeting reduce the scope for welfare-improving nitpicking.

工作介绍Overview

本文考察一种常见的保险欺诈情形：投保人遭遇了保单不予承保的真实损失，却故意将其谎报为承保索赔。研究发现，当欺诈调查成本较低时，投保人倾向于购买较少的保险；反之，当调查成本较高时，他们会购买更多的保险。我们还研究了预审计算法如何辅助传统人工审计：只有在其检测灵敏度足够高时，这项技术才有助于遏制欺诈；一旦灵敏度低于某一阈值，部署该算法反而可能弊大于利，此时不如不使用。最后，我们研究了一种事后审计工具——"吹毛求疵式"理赔处理（nitpicking），即保险公司对理赔进行极其细致的审查以削减赔付金额。这种方式虽然能提高审计的经济收益并遏制造假，但随着预理赔算法灵敏度的提升，它变得不再必要。

We examine a common type of insurance fraud where a policyholder experiences a real loss that is not covered by their policy, but deliberately reports it as a covered claim. Our research finds that when the cost of investigating fraud is low, policyholders tend to buy less insurance. Conversely, when investigation costs are high, they purchase more insurance. We also look at how a pre-auditing algorithm can support traditional manual auditing. Our findings show that this technology only helps deter fraud if its detection sensitivity is sufficiently high. If the sensitivity falls below a certain threshold, implementing the algorithm can actually do more harm than good, making it better to avoid using it. Finally, we study a post-audit tool, referred to as “nitpicking”, where insurers conduct extremely thorough reviews to reduce payout amounts. While this approach can increase the financial benefits of auditing and deter fraud, it becomes unnecessary as the sensitivity of the pre-claim algorithm improves.
